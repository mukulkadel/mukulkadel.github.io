I'm Mukul Kadel, a software developer. This blog is where I write about the things I run into day to day — programming patterns, system design, networking internals, databases, and the odd Unix tool I end up relying on.

Most posts are practical: a command that actually worked, a concept that finally clicked once I saw it explained a certain way, or a walkthrough of something I built. The goal is to write the post I wish I'd found when I was looking something up.
