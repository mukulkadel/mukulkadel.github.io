This page explains what data this site collects and how it's used. mukulkadel.com is a personal blog; it doesn't require account registration and doesn't collect personal information directly beyond what's described below.

## Analytics

This site uses **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on page, and general traffic sources. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information anonymously. You can opt out using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Advertising

This site shows ads served by **Google AdSense**. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. This may include personalized advertising depending on your settings.

- You can review and adjust how Google uses your data for ad personalization at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- More detail on how Google uses data when you use partner sites is available at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, ad partners may rely on the [Google-certified consent management platform framework](https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/).

## Cookies

Cookies set on this site are used for analytics and ad delivery as described above. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how ads and analytics behave but won't prevent you from reading the site.

Posts on this site sometimes link to external sites, tools, or affiliate programs (e.g. Amazon). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this site isn't responsible for their content or practices.
