--- layout: app title: QR Code Generator permalink: /apps/qr-generator/ description: Generate a QR code from any text or URL instantly in your browser, with no external service involved. ---

Encoded entirely in your browser with a small built-in QR encoder (byte mode, error correction level M, versions 1-6 — up to 106 bytes) — nothing is sent to a server. For longer input, use a dedicated QR tool that supports higher versions.
